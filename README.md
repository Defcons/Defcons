# Martin Davidsen

**Software & control-systems engineer** based in **Norway** 🇳🇴 — founder of **[Agentas](https://agentas.net)**.
I work across two worlds: **industrial automation & critical control systems** by profession, and
**full-stack web apps, developer tooling, and self-hosted infrastructure** by craft.

Professionally I have **15+ years in tech**, with close to a decade in **industrial control systems**
across the **oil & gas and energy** industries — from offshore platforms and subsea control to
operator tools — covering the full lifecycle from design and factory testing through on-site startup
and production support. That work is safety-critical and doesn't live on GitHub, but it shapes how I
build everything else.

I care deeply about **safety and security**: I favour secure-by-default designs, careful handling of
data and access, and software that's tested and dependable enough to run in production. For me
**AI isn't a buzzword — it's how I work every day**: most of my projects are an ongoing experiment in
**AI-assisted ("agentic") software engineering**, directing AI coding agents to design, build, and
ship real software end to end.

## 🔭 Featured work

- **[clouddrive](https://github.com/Defcons/clouddrive)** — self-hosted file storage with a **Go**
  backend and **React + TypeScript** frontend: authentication & MFA, share links, granular
  permissions, trash/restore, and audit logging, packaged with **Docker**.
- **[no-bs](https://github.com/Defcons/no-bs)** ([nobs.agentas.net](https://nobs.agentas.net)) — a
  no-nonsense **workout log**: sets, reps, kg, a rest timer, heart rate — local-first, no account, no
  subscription. **React + TypeScript** PWA, packaged for Android with **Capacitor**.
- **[assistkey](https://github.com/Defcons/assistkey)** — a **Windows system-tray push-to-talk
  client** for **Home Assistant** Assist: hold a hotkey, speak, get an answer. DPAPI-encrypted token
  at rest, optional wake word, shipped as a single-file executable with CI.

I spend most of my spare time on **self-hosting and home infrastructure** — a hardened Proxmox/Docker
homelab (secure-by-default, deny-all firewall, Tailscale-only access), automating deployments, and
building small tools to tie it all together.
